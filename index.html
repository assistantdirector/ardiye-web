static monolith

wrk // 001

archive info

mixed media spatial installation documenting industrial textures.

  • artistkaan d.
  • mediumraw concrete, iron, 16mm film
  • year2024
  • edition1 of 1 (site-specific)

static monolith acts as a tactile exploration of industrial decay and permanence. casting raw concrete against corrugated sheet metal, the piece retains the microscopic fractures of the foundry space.

the physical installation is accompanied by continuous 16mm archival loops projecting light onto the oxidised textures, examining the tension between structural weight and transient light.
