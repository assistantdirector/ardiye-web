theory & manifesto

ref: man-01

ardiye is a physical entity and documentation center centered entirely around artistic labor. utilizing an academic methodology, it records the raw, unhewn stages of the production process.

rejecting the sterile, isolated distance of traditional "white cube" galleries, our focus remains on material resistance, the rigid mathematics of the editing table, and the intellectual foundation behind the craftsmanship.
