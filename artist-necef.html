necef mengüverdi is a visual artist investigating the physical retention of memory within raw environments.

her practice explores the intersection of spatial architecture and material resistance. by observing how natural and artificial elements degrade, adapt, and fuse over time, she documents the fragmented dialogues established by decaying forms.

refusing the concept of a "finished piece," her works function as continuous experiments—exposing the underlying tension between structural permanence and inevitable erosion.
