nazım kahramantürk is a filmmaker, director, and post-production editor whose work revolves around the mechanical and structural aspects of digital storytelling.

with a formal foundation in film design and direction, his practice focuses heavily on raw cinematography, intricate multi-camera directing systems, and the disintegration of digital spatial memory.

operating at the intersection of creative direction and technical workflow architecture, his methodology treats the editing timeline as a physical construction site. beyond visual framing, his approach extends into software utility; he independently developed and deployed trackmatte, a multi-platform application engineered specifically to streamline vfx tracking processes.
