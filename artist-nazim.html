nazım kahramantürk is a filmmaker and director focusing on raw, unpolished visual storytelling and complex multi-camera production setups.

operating heavily within post-production environments, his work emphasizes structural integrity, rigorous color grading pipelines, and the mechanical discipline of digital editing.

as a co-founder of ardiyé, he directs the collective's moving image archive and spatial documentation projects.
