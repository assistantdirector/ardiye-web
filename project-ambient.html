a study of how sound shapes empty spaces, mapped onto raw industrial footage.

ambient architecture uses resonant frequencies to physically vibrate the immediate environment, translating invisible acoustic waves into a tangible architectural presence.

the accompanying visual projections are entirely reactive, distorting in real-time according to the amplitude and pitch of the surrounding soundscape.
